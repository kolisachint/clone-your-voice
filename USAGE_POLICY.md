# Usage policy

clone-your-voice lets you make a copy of **your own** voice so you can use it on your own computer. This policy says what that is for, and what it is not for.

## The short version

- Use it with **your own voice**, or with a voice whose owner has **explicitly given you permission**.
- Do not use it to pretend to be someone else, to commit fraud, to harass anyone, to deceive voters, or to make content a person did not agree to.

## What you may do

- Train a voice pack from recordings of yourself.
- Make speech with your voice for your own purposes: notes, messages, accessibility, creative work, and so on.
- Train from another person's voice **only** when they have clearly agreed, and you keep a record of that agreement.

## What you may not do

- **Impersonation:** make a voice that sounds like a real person (a friend, colleague, public figure, family member) to make others think that person said something they did not say.
- **Fraud:** use a cloned voice to trick someone into sending money, data, passwords, or access, including "family emergency" and "boss" scams.
- **Harassment:** make audio meant to threaten, intimidate, insult, or humiliate anyone.
- **Political deception:** make audio that misrepresents what a candidate, official, or party said or did, or that is meant to mislead voters.
- **Non-consensual content:** make sexual, intimate, or degrading audio of a real person without their clear consent.
- **Hiding origin:** remove or get around the watermark to pass off generated audio as a real recording of a person.

## Consent record

When you prepare a dataset, the tool asks you to type a one-line statement that the voice is yours or that you have permission. That statement is saved in the voice pack as a record.

The record is **not enforcement**. The tool cannot check who is speaking, and a file can be edited by anyone. The record exists so that you, and anyone you share a pack with, have a clear statement of intent. You are still responsible for what you do.

## Watermark

Generated audio includes an inaudible watermark by default. It is a provenance signal: it helps show that audio was made by a tool like this one. You may turn it off with `--no-watermark`. Turning it off does **not** change your responsibilities under this policy. A watermark is not a guarantee. Some edits and compression can remove it, so do not rely on it to prove where audio came from.

## Cloud training and third-party terms

Training runs on Google Colab, and Colab has its own terms. Some of those terms ban creating deepfakes, and the free tier limits some kinds of use. You are responsible for following Google's terms and the terms of any other service you use. This project does not make a use acceptable under those terms.

## Reporting misuse

If you see this tool, a voice pack, or a shared sample being used to harm someone, please report it to the maintainers through a GitHub issue or, for private matters, through the private security advisory form described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Changes

This policy may change as the project changes. The version in the default branch of the repository applies.
